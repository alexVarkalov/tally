# Texts for @BotFather. Keep in sync with tally/handlers/__init__.py and tally/i18n.py (help_commands).

## /setcommands

menu - pick a tracker and a day
stats - this month, all trackers
trackers - list trackers
new - create a tracker: /new <key> [label]
attach - attach a worksheet: /attach <key> <worksheet> [label]
rename - change a label: /rename <key> <label>
archive - hide a tracker: /archive <key>
unarchive - show a tracker again: /unarchive <key>
detach - remove from the registry: /detach <key> confirm
timezone - set your timezone: /timezone Europe/Warsaw
help - what this bot does

## /setdescription

Tally records events per day into a Google Sheet, one worksheet per tracker, and shows monthly summaries. Tap a tracker, then a day. Private bot.

## /setabouttext

Private tally bot: one tap per event, one worksheet per tracker, monthly stats from the sheet.
